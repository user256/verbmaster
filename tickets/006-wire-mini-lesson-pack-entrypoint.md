# 006 - Wire mini-lesson pack entrypoint

## Goal

Expose mini-lesson packs through a simple index entrypoint for discovery.

## Scope

- Add lesson references to `mini-lessons/index.md`.
- Ensure naming follows `NNN-slug` format.
- Ensure entry points to the generated pack file.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Village life pack is listed in the mini-lesson index.
- Index path references a real pack file.

## Dependencies

- `001-create-mini-lesson-structure.md`
- `002-ingest-village-life-source.md`
