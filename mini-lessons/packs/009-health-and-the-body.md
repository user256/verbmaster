  en: I don't feel very well today.

  en: I've had a cold for the last two days.
- es: ¿Tiene algo para el dolor de garganta?

  en: I think I need to make an appointment with the doctor.

  en: You have to rest and drink plenty of water.
- es: No es nada grave, tómatelo con calma y que te mejores.
  en: It's nothing serious, take it easy and get well soon.
